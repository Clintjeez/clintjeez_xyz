## May 2026

### Restructured from 8 services to 3 tiers
**Decision:** Replaced the 8-service lifecycle menu with 3 clear tiers: $366 Sprint, $3,500 Build, $1,333/mo Retainer.
**Why:** 8 services caused decision paralysis for prospects. The 3-tier model has a clear entry point and natural upgrade path.
**Note:** The 8 lifecycle systems still exist as internal frameworks, content pillars, and case study material — they're not gone, just not the primary offer presentation.

### Launched 48-hour free sprint campaign
**Decision:** Run 3–5 free sprints for SaaS founders in exchange for video testimonials and case studies.
**Why:** No case studies = hard to close $3,500 paid builds. Proof layer first, then charge full price.
**Success criteria:** 3 published case studies with real numbers, 3 video testimonials.

### Set content flywheel as top priority
**Decision:** 2 blog articles/day, 6 social posts/day (3 LinkedIn + 3 X), 1 YouTube script/week.
**Why:** Content is the primary client acquisition channel. Volume builds SEO surface area, authority, and inbound pipeline simultaneously.
**Risk:** Volume vs. quality tension. Mitigation: the repurpose engine reduces new writing per day — one article becomes 7 pieces.

### Pricing model locked
**Decision:** $366 Sprint / $3,500 Build / $1,333/mo Retainer.
**Why:** Specific non-round numbers signal deliberate pricing. All three undercut agency rates by 60–80% while delivering senior-level execution. Entry point ($366) is low enough to be impulse-buy, high enough to filter serious leads.
