## May 2026

### Restructured from 8 services to 3 tiers
**Decision:** Replaced the 8-service lifecycle menu with 3 clear tiers: $366 Sprint, $3,500 Build, $1,333/mo Retainer.
**Why:** 8 services caused decision paralysis for prospects. The 3-tier model has a clear entry point and natural upgrade path.
**Note:** The 8 lifecycle systems still exist as internal frameworks, content pillars, and case study material — they're not gone, just not the primary offer presentation.

### Launched 48-hour free sprint campaign
**Decision:** Run 3–5 free sprints for SaaS founders in exchange for video testimonials and case studies.
**Why:** No case studies = hard to close $3,500 paid builds. Proof layer first, then charge full price.
**Success criteria:** 3 published case studies with real numbers, 3 video testimonials.

### Set content flywheel as top priority
**Decision:** 2 blog articles/day, 6 social posts/day (3 LinkedIn + 3 X), 1 YouTube script/week.
**Why:** Content is the primary client acquisition channel. Volume builds SEO surface area, authority, and inbound pipeline simultaneously.
**Risk:** Volume vs. quality tension. Mitigation: the repurpose engine reduces new writing per day — one article becomes 7 pieces.

### Pricing model locked
**Decision:** $366 Sprint / $3,500 Build / $1,333/mo Retainer.
**Why:** Specific non-round numbers signal deliberate pricing. All three undercut agency rates by 60–80% while delivering senior-level execution. Entry point ($366) is low enough to be impulse-buy, high enough to filter serious leads.

### Adopted the 3-Lever Offer Framework for all outreach
**Decision:** All cold outreach now runs on `context/offer-framework.md` — Lever 1 (Service Lead Magnet, a free derivative mapped to one of the 8 growth systems), Lever 2 (Guarantee, sales-call-only for great-fit prospects), Lever 3 (Easy Yes, a 3-min Loom teardown for volume outreach).
**Why:** Generic "free consultation"/"free audit" offers don't start real conversations. A pre-built derivative or bounded micro-deliverable proves diagnosis-before-prescription in the outreach itself instead of just claiming it.
**Note:** The 48-hour free sprint campaign (`content/campaigns/48hr-sprint/`) stays a separate, time-boxed proof-layer play — it gives away the whole Tier 1 service for case studies, not a derivative. Once its 5 slots fill, all outreach reverts to this framework. `sops/cold-email.md` updated to route through it.

## October 2026

### Repositioned from Growth & GTM Engineer to AI Engineer, with FDE roles as the main path
**Decision:** Changed the title to "AI Engineer — Growth & GTM Systems." Landing a Forward Deployed Engineer role is now the primary goal. The consulting tiers ($366 / $3,500 / $1,333/mo) are now supporting proof, not the main revenue path.
**Why:** Focus has shifted to building AI/ML projects and applying to FDE roles. Recruiters search for "AI Engineer" and "Forward Deployed," and "Growth" first reads as a marketer. FDE work (embed with a customer, diagnose, ship, prove the result) maps directly onto 9 years of client-facing delivery, which is the scarce half most FDE candidates lack.
**Note:** Growth stays in the title as the specialty, because it sets him apart in a crowded AI Engineer field. Updated so far: site title/meta/JSON-LD/hero, `context/me.md`, CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md, `context/goals.md` (north star: FDE offer by April 2027), `context/current-priorities.md` (proof projects + application pipeline). Still to rework: content pillars, SEO keyword clusters, services framing, brand voice, LinkedIn/X bios.
**Risk:** The title is only a claim until there are 2–3 shipped AI/ML projects with real depth (agents, RAG, evals, deployed, measured). Proof projects come before volume content.

### Offers section and free sprint reframed around AI deployment
**Decision:** The homepage now goes Hero → Work → What I Deploy → Blog. The 8 growth-system cards are replaced by 4 AI deployment cards (AI Agents, Knowledge Assistants, Predictive Models, AI Workflow Automation). The free sprint is now the "48-Hour AI Deployment Sprint," which also asks for permission to publish an anonymized case study. Removed the unused `PricingPlans.tsx`.
**Why:** Offers sat right below the hero, so recruiters saw a GTM consulting menu first. Each sprint now works as a real FDE-style deployment that produces portfolio proof.
**Note:** The 8 growth systems remain as domain knowledge and content material. `cold-dm-playbook.md` scripts still use the growth-sprint pitch and need rewriting.
