# SOP: Cold Email (Clinton's Own Outreach)

For Clinton's outreach to win clients — not for building client deliverables.

## ICP Targeting

See `context/icp.md` for full profiles. Priority target: Early-Stage SaaS Founders ($0–$500K ARR) showing visible growth plateau signals.

## How to Spot a Good Prospect

- Active on LinkedIn or X, posting about growth problems
- Product is live with real users but growth is stalled
- Has tried one-off tactics (Product Hunt launch, paid ads) that didn't compound
- Company is 6–24 months old — past the build phase, stuck in the grow phase

## Email #1 — The Opener

**Subject:** 7 words max. Specific + benefit-implied. No clickbait.
Examples:
- "Quick question about [Company]'s activation funnel"
- "Noticed something on [Company]'s pricing page"
- "[Name] — one question about your onboarding"

**Body structure:**
1. One-line personalization (specific to them — not "I love what you're building")
2. What you noticed (the diagnosis — a specific visible problem signal)
3. What you do about it (one sentence — not a pitch deck)
4. Soft CTA (15-min call OR just reply with one question)

**Length:** Under 100 words. Always.

**Lead with:** The $366 Growth Sprint. Never open with the $3,500 Build.

## Follow-Up Sequence

| Day | Email | Angle |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Opener | Diagnosis + Sprint offer |
| 4 | Follow-up 1 | Different angle on the same problem |
| 8 | Follow-up 2 | "Closing the loop" — last attempt |
| 30+ | Re-engage | Share a relevant article or case study |

## Tone Rules

- Direct. Never apologize for reaching out.
- Show you've done your homework (1 specific detail about their company).
- One idea per email. Never list all your services.
- Never use "I hope this email finds you well."
