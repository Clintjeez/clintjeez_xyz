# SOP: Client Proposal

## For: The 48-Hour Growth Sprint ($366)

One page. Max. Sent as a PDF or clean markdown.

**Structure:**
1. **What I noticed** — 1 specific diagnosis of their business (not generic, not flattery)
2. **What we'll do in 48 hours** — 1 specific deliverable (name it exactly)
3. **What this fixes** — the outcome in their terms (business language, not technical)
4. **Investment** — $366. No negotiation framing. State it clean.
5. **Next step** — Book + pay link. One action.

---

## For: The Growth System Build ($3,500)

Two pages max.

**Structure:**
1. **Diagnosis summary** — 2–3 specific bottlenecks identified from the discovery call
2. **What we'll build** — 3 named systems mapped to their lifecycle stage (use names from `context/services.md`)
3. **Timeline** — 3–4 weeks with milestone breakdown (Week 1: diagnosis + instrumentation, Week 2–3: builds, Week 4: handoff + documentation)
4. **Investment** — $3,500 flat. $1,750 upfront, $1,750 on delivery.
5. **What's included** — Deliverables list (be specific)
6. **What's not included** — Scope boundary (prevents scope creep)
7. **Next step** — Sign + first payment link

---

## For: The Growth Engine Retainer ($1,333/mo)

One page.

**Structure:**
1. **What's working** — Summary of results from the Build engagement
2. **What we optimize each month** — 2–3 growth areas with specific focus
3. **Monthly deliverables** — What they get every month (report, experiments, implementations)
4. **Investment** — $1,333/mo. Cancel anytime. No lock-in.
5. **Next step** — Start date + first payment link

---

## Tone Rules for All Proposals

- Name the specific problem first. Always. Before anything else.
- Outcome-first language: "By the end of week 3, you'll have X running" not "we will work on X"
- No fluff. No agency buzzwords. No "synergy" or "holistic approach."
- Never apologize for the price. State it and move on.
- Short paragraphs. Scannable. They'll read it on their phone.

## After Sending

Follow up in 48 hours if no response.
Single line: "Any questions on the proposal? Happy to jump on a 10-min call."
Do not resend the proposal. Do not discount unless they give a specific objection.
