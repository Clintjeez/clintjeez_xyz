# Customer LifeCycle

The Hook
$0 to $20k monthly revenue wont just happen for that your business or SaaS company.
Customer lifecycle is the foundation every SaaS product growth strategy is either built on or quietly collapsing without.

The Promise

This is the core growth framework every founder should take seriously. by the end of this video you will understand why and how important every stages of the customer lifeCycle are.

The problem

Growth is not all about traffic and more traffic, building and growing as a founder without understanding your customer LifeCycle is like sailing the sea without a compass.
Every customer that ever gave you money went through eight stages to get there:
they discovered you,
they clicked through,
they signed up,
they experienced your product,
they converted to paid,
they stayed,
they expanded and
some of them referred others.

The Framework
The eight stages of the customer lifecycle are:
Discovery or Awareness - here the customer learns you exist ,

Acquisition - here the customer take the first step to sign-up, book a demo

Activation - here the customer experience your core value,

Conversion - here the customer move from free to paid, trial to subscription or prospect to customer,

Retention - at this stage your product has become part of their workflow,

Revenue Expansion - at this stage they want more of your offers and pay to upgrade depeneding on your pricing model. in B2B SaaS this means moving from premium user to an enterprise user,

Referral or Advocacy - at this stage they tell someone about your product and convince them to join,

and Reactivation - at this stage you bring the customer back into the lifecycle after they churned or you have got new features and offers.

The customer lifecycle isn't a concept. It's the system your business either has or doesn't have.

And the moment you stop asking "how do I get more customers" and start asking "where in this eight-stage system is my revenue actually stuck" — that's when growth stops feeling like guesswork.
