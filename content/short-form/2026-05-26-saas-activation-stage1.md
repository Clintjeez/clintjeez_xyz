## THE DIAGNOSIS (1min–2min 30s)

*[Structured. Call out each step clearly. Use fingers to count.]*

Here's the 3-step activation audit. which you can run today if you have a digital analytics setup for your product .

**Step 1 — Find your best users.**
Go into PostHog or Mixpanel. Pull the cohort of users who signed up 90 days ago and are still active and paying. What did they do in their first session? First 48 hours? List those actions in order. That sequence is your activation path.

**Step 2 — Find where churned users fall off.**
Now pull the opposite cohort — signed up 90 days ago, churned in month one. Overlay their first-session behavior against your successful users. Find the step where the paths diverge. That single divergence point is your activation bottleneck. It's almost always one specific moment.

**Step 3 — Remove one friction from that step.**
This is rarely a product change. Usually it's one of three things: a behaviorally-triggered email that fires the moment a user stalls at that step, a short Loom walkthrough sent when they hit that wall, or removing one required field that most users can't complete without help.

## REAL EXAMPLE (2min 30s–3min 30s)

*[Slow down. This is the proof.]*

I worked with a B2B SaaS founder. $8,000 a month on LinkedIn ads. 200 signups. 14 paid customers.

His instinct: fix the ads.

The diagnosis said something different.

His activation rate was 22%. Step 4 of 7 in his onboarding required an API integration that most users didn't have credentials ready for. 78% of his signups were dying at that one step.

We built one thing: a Loom walkthrough that fired automatically when a user stalled at step 4.

45 days later — activation moved from 22% to 38%. Same ad spend. Same traffic. 14 paid customers became 27.
