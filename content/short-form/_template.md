## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Record in one take where possible — edits break the flow on short-form
- No b-roll needed for talking head; clean background, good lighting
- Captions are non-negotiable — 85% of short-form is watched on mute
- First frame matters: don't start on black or a wide shot — face fills the frame immediately
- Post natively to each platform — don't cross-post from TikTok (watermark kills reach)
