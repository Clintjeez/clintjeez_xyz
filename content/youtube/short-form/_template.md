# Short-Form Script Template

*Type: [Single Insight / Contrarian Take / Tactical Tip / Clipped Moment]*
*Pillar: [1–5]*
*Source: [Blog article slug, long-form video filename, or "original concept"]*
*Date: YYYY-MM-DD*
*Format: [Talking Head, vertical 9:16]*
*Target runtime: [Under 60s — 30–45s is the sweet spot]*

Short-form scripts live here: `content/youtube/short-form/`. These are single-platform-agnostic scripts, postable as YouTube Shorts, IG Reels, or TikTok with the same cut. One idea per script — no multi-part breakdowns. For a full 6–10 min video, use `content/youtube/long-form/_template.md` instead.

## HOOK (0–3s)

[The first line, spoken over the first frame. Must work with sound off too — text overlay should carry the claim on its own.]

## PAYOFF (3–45s)

[One idea, delivered fast. No throat-clearing, no "so basically," no setup — go straight into the mechanism.]

[If this is clipped from a long-form video or blog article, this is the single sharpest insight from that source — not a summary of the whole thing.]

[End on the line most likely to be repeated or quoted in a comment.]

## CTA (last 3–5s)

[One line only. Pick one:]
- "Follow for the rest of this breakdown."
- "Full framework — link in bio."
- "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you the template."
