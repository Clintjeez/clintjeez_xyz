# Cold DM Playbook — 48-Hour Free Sprint Campaign

**Goal:** Book 3–5 free 48-hour growth sprints via LinkedIn and X DMs.
**Exchange:** Free 15-min audit + 48-hour implementation → Loom testimonial + written case study.
**ICP:** Early-stage SaaS founder, $0–$200K ARR, active on LinkedIn/X, visible growth problem.

---

## How to Identify Prospects

Before sending any DM, qualify the person. They must show at least one of:

- Posting about a growth problem (activation, churn, pipeline, outbound)
- Building in public with visible stall signals (plateau posts, "struggling with X")
- Recently launched on Product Hunt or Indie Hackers — typical post-launch plateau
- Actively asking for advice in comments or threads
- Bio says "founder" + "SaaS" or "building X"

**Do not DM:**
- Accounts with no recent activity (last 30+ days silent)
- Large companies (10+ team members visible)
- Marketers, agencies, consultants — they're not the ICP
- Anyone who already follows you (they know you exist — don't cold DM, engage instead)

## The 15-Min Audit — What Happens on the Call

When they say yes, frame the call clearly before they join:

**Pre-call message (send when scheduling):**
```
Here's what we'll cover in 15 minutes:

1. Quick snapshot of where you are (ARR, signups, activation rate)
2. I'll point at the single highest-leverage bottleneck
3. If it's fixable in 48 hours → I'll build it for you, free
4. All I ask: a 3-5 min Loom testimonial if it works

Booking link: cal.com/clintonjames/15min
```

**On the call — diagnosis framework:**
- Ask: Where are users dropping off? (acquisition → activation → retention)
- Ask: What's the current activation rate? (ideally < 40% means activation is broken)
- Ask: What have they already tried?
- Identify: the single bottleneck that, if fixed, creates the most downstream impact
- Commit: state clearly what you'll build in 48 hours

## The 48-Hour Sprint — What You Deliver

Pick one of these based on the diagnosis:

| Bottleneck Found | 48-Hour Deliverable |
|------------------|---------------------|
| No onboarding sequence | 3-email onboarding drip written + set up in their ESP |
| Broken activation flow | New in-app checklist design + copy, wired if in a no-code tool |
| Cold outreach not working | 3-sequence cold email + Clay enrichment setup + domain check |
| No lead magnet / top of funnel | One lead magnet outlined + landing page copy |
| Churn with no early warning | PostHog/Mixpanel event taxonomy + 2 retention alerts defined |
| Pricing page not converting | Full CRO audit + rewrite of pricing page copy |

**Rule:** Ship one thing. One working thing beats a 20-slide deck.
