# Ideal Customer Profiles

## Primary ICP — Early-Stage SaaS Founder

**Title:** Founder / CEO
**Company:** B2B or B2C SaaS
**Stage:** $0–$500K ARR, post-product/market fit, pre-Series A
**Team size:** 1–10 people
**Problem:** Has users but can't reliably grow them. Activation is broken, churn is invisible, acquisition is manual and inconsistent.
**Budget mindset:** Project-based, cautious. Needs to see ROI fast before committing to retainers.
**Watering holes:** LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, niche Slack communities
**Trigger to buy:** Has tried DIY growth tactics. They didn't compound. Ready for a system.

---

## Secondary ICP — Indie Hacker / Solo Operator

**Title:** Founder / Builder
**Revenue:** $1K–$20K MRR
**Problem:** Wearing every hat. Growth is inconsistent. Content is sporadic. No repeatable system.
**Budget mindset:** Lean. Will pay for things that directly produce revenue — not strategy decks.
**Watering holes:** Indie Hackers, X/Twitter, Reddit (r/indiehackers, r/SaaS), YouTube
**Trigger to buy:** Revenue plateau. Something that was working stopped working.

---

## Secondary ICP — SME Owner (B2B Services / Agency)

**Title:** Founder / Director / MD
**Company:** 5–50 person services business — agency, consultancy, professional services
**Problem:** Pipeline is referral-dependent. No repeatable outbound or inbound system. Growth is random.
**Budget mindset:** ROI-driven. Pays for outcomes, not activity reports.
**Watering holes:** LinkedIn, industry associations, local business networks
**Trigger to buy:** Lost a key client. Realized the pipeline is dangerously thin.

---

## Secondary ICP — Web3 / Crypto Brand

**Title:** Founder / CMO / Growth Lead
**Company:** DeFi protocol, NFT project, Web3 SaaS, blockchain startup
**Problem:** Community growth is chaotic. No lifecycle system. Product adoption is low outside the core community bubble.
**Budget mindset:** Often well-funded (pre-token or post-raise) but short on operational thinking.
**Watering holes:** X/Twitter, Discord, Telegram, Farcaster, crypto-specific Substacks
**Trigger to buy:** Token launch didn't drive retention. Community is stagnating. Needs a growth system.

---

## Anti-ICP — Who to Avoid

- **Large enterprise (500+ employees)** — procurement cycles kill deal momentum
- **Pre-product founders** — can't diagnose what isn't built yet
- **"Viral content" seekers** — want tricks, not systems; will churn on the sprint
- **Hourly billing requesters** — misaligned with the flat-price model
- **Founders who want a co-founder** — that's a different engagement than productized services
